## v1.4.9 Highlights
- New: visual Stock Report page — what your shelves hold and what they are worth
- Total cost of stock, retail value, estimated profit with a margin bar, low/out-of-stock chips, and a Where-the-money-sits per-product breakdown with bars
- Open it from Reports > Stock; Manage stock stays one tap away; recomputed live on every open and pull-to-refresh

## v1.4.8 Highlights
- Fix: sales and expenses can no longer vanish from Today, the Day/Week/Month/Year reports or the dashboard when the phone's clock is corrected — data written while the clock ran ahead is shifted back automatically (order preserved, nothing lost), even if the license is revoked, expired or resumed
- Reports and the dashboard self-heal before every computation and refresh on open, on app resume and via pull-to-refresh

Install: download the APK on the phone and open it (allow installing from unknown sources when prompted).
